# Coho 2025 Gamelist (not a ranking b/c I can't be bothered)
-----
## Game(s) of the year
-----
* MegaBonk - A Tier: Very fun mashup of Risk of Rain 2 and Vamp Survivors + memes
	* <https://store.steampowered.com/app/3405340/Megabonk/>
* Easy Transportation Inc.- S Tier: Just exceptional, fun short gameplay experience
	* <https://store.steampowered.com/app/3293010/Easy_Delivery_Co/>
* Shadows of Doubt: A Tier: REALLY cool detective game with procedural quest system.  a bit too wonky to be S tier, but very worth playing.
	* <https://store.steampowered.com/app/986130/Shadows_of_Doubt/>

-----
## Honorable Mentions
-----
* Cyberpunk 2077 + Phantom Liberty: A+ Experience, Very good and immersive world Loved it.
	* <https://store.steampowered.com/app/1091500/Cyberpunk_2077/>
* MonsterHunter Wilds: B Tier- A lot of the spikiness I loved of MH is gone here but I need to go back and try out the patches
	* <https://store.steampowered.com/app/2246340/Monster_Hunter_Wilds/>
* Ostranauts: A+ Tier - Super interesting and deep (but rough) space salvaging survival game. Really love the attention to detail
	* <https://store.steampowered.com/app/1022980/Ostranauts/>
* Factorio: SpaceAge: SSS Tier: Just exceptional.  Probably up there for favorite game of all time and the expansion kept pace amazingly
	* <https://store.steampowered.com/app/645390/Factorio_Space_Age/>
* Cast n Chill: A Tier: Very chill idle game, amazing art and fun simple fishing, but needs WAY more content/progression.
	* <https://store.steampowered.com/app/3483740/Cast_n_Chill/>
* Hackmud: B+ Tier hacking game, very immersive and nerdy terminal-based MMO? very cool concept but just a bit too inaccessible, though perhaps thats the point
	* <https://store.steampowered.com/app/469920/hackmud/>
* Tunnet: B Tier networking game: Yeah you network hardware and dig out new devices to build a network that's efficient, but it's a bit too steep in progression/complexity IMO
	* <https://store.steampowered.com/app/2286390/Tunnet/>
* A Short Hike: SS Tier adventure game, just amazing adventure game.  Top music, fun retro art, and completable in like 2 hrs
	* <https://store.steampowered.com/app/1055540/A_Short_Hike/>
* Ixion: C+ tier city builder: This game just is a bit to difficult in the wrong tedious ways. Great concept though!
	* <https://store.steampowered.com/app/1113120/IXION/>
* Shadow Empire: A Tier 4x game:  SUPER deep systems, cool setting, could use more UX and polish in experience/animations/explanations.  Very worth though.
	* <https://store.steampowered.com/app/1154840/Shadow_Empire/>

-----
## Games I went back to
-----
* Eve Online: A Tier MMO (if you're into the long haul).  Has hit some great improvements in the past couple years so enjoying getting back into it.
	* <https://store.steampowered.com/app/8500/EVE_Online/>
* Stardew Valley: SSS Tier farming game ofc.  You know
	* <https://store.steampowered.com/app/413150/Stardew_Valley/>
* Hardspace Shipbreaker: SS Tier chill break-apart spaceship game + cool story. Can't wait for more. this was my 2nd playthrough
	* <https://store.steampowered.com/app/1161580/Hardspace_Shipbreaker/>
* Webfishing: B+-A tier fishing game. Cool progression and just amazing vibes and content design.  Love it and wish It wasn't abandoned but man... so good
	* <https://store.steampowered.com/app/3146520/WEBFISHING/>
